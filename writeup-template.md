# Writeup Template

Copy this file, fill in the block at the top, and write the sections below.

## About the block at the top

Those fields are read by monsec.io. A writeup merged into this repository is
published to the site automatically, and the fields decide how it appears:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| `author` | Shown as the author of the article. Your name, however you want it written. |
| `date` | The date on the article, as `YYYY-MM-DD`. Leave it out and the article shows no date, which is better than a wrong one. |
| `competition` | Becomes a tag, so a whole CTF can be browsed as a set. |
| `category` | Becomes a tag. Web, Crypto, Pwn, Rev, Misc, Blockchain, Forensics, OSINT. |
| `difficulty` | Becomes a tag. Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard. |
| `challenge_author` | Credit for whoever wrote the challenge. Not the same person as you. |
| `canonical` | If you posted this somewhere else first, put that link here and the article will say "also available on" and point at it. Leave it empty otherwise. |

Anything you leave out is simply absent from the article. Nothing breaks, and
nothing is guessed at on your behalf.

## Images

Put screenshots in the repository next to the writeup, or link them. Either
way monsec.io downloads a copy when it publishes, so the writeup keeps working
even if the original host goes away. That is not hypothetical: every screenshot
in the DUCTF 2023 writeups was hotlinked to one person's Medium account.
